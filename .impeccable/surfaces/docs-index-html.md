# Surface brief: the public calendar (`docs/index.html`)

Scope: the calendar home page and the public pages that share its world (`/e/<id>`, `/submit`, `/thisweek`). Mode: Operate. Build path this session: code-led (the only artwork is the designer's logo, so nothing is generated).

Audience and job: someone in Memphis on a phone deciding what to see tonight or this weekend; DJs reading the list on air. Task: scan by day, narrow by neighborhood or search, open a show, add it to a calendar or buy a ticket. Constraints: sponsor banner, fund-drive banner and dated sponsor callouts stay visible; every tripwire in `CLAUDE.md` holds; class names and ids the browser suites drive are kept.

Unresolved: admin pages keep the old slate look; the co-brand header image (WYXR + Where's the Music Memphis) is replaced by a text credit; mascot and wordmark cut-downs are derived from the flattened logo until the designer supplies separations.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the front of a neighborhood theater, printed in the logo's three inks. Tonight's bill is up on the marquee; the days after it hang beneath as tear-off calendar leaves, the same leaf the mascot is holding. It refuses the dark event-app grid of thumbnail cards under a gradient hero.

OWN-WORLD: Cream stock ground, tomato-red keylines and fills, marigold only where something is lit (a pick, an active filter, the marquee bulbs), white leaves, a red-brown ink for reading text. Flat ink: 2px keylines, no shadows, no gradients, corners rounded like the logo's URL badge. Marquee band with a row of bulbs; ruled tracks between acts; date leaves with a red month band and binding holes; picks cut like tickets. Irregardless caps for dates and marquee words, its weight axis carrying state; Anybody caps for act names; Inter for everything else.

STORY: The visitor sees what is on tonight before anything else, reads forward day by day, narrows the list without losing the counts, opens a show, and leaves with a calendar entry or a ticket.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: partner credit strip; masthead with the mascot at left and CONCERT CALENDAR set large beside the Memphis script; sticky bar holding the month switcher (which opens the month grid), search and neighborhood chips; then the Tonight marquee with its first acts. Desktop: sticky left rail with the full logo lockup, the month grid for jumping to a day, neighborhoods as a counted list, search, submit and subscribe; the marquee and leaves hold a readable measure to its right, each day with its leaf in a rail of its own.

FORM: marquee letter board (4 of 7 on the grounded list; seed e0e85c29), carried in the brief-pinned logo world: the tear-off leaf and the ticket come from the mascot's hands. Raises: weight as state (variable-font specimen); marigold rationed to lit things (monochrome product marketing); times in a tight tabular column (cassette J-card); the list keeps its measure and the margin takes the extra width (centre-rail reference); filters never hide the counts (transit diagram).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
